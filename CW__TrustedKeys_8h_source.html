9/******************************************************************
10 * Cryptnox trusted CA public keys for offline certificate verification.
11 *
12 * Source: https://verify.cryptnox.tech/certificates/
13 *
14 * Each key is stored as 64 raw bytes: X[32] || Y[32] (secp256r1,
15 * no 0x04 prefix), suitable for direct use with uECC_verify().
16 *
17 * To update a key: download the .crt file from the URL above and
18 * extract the EC public key coordinates with:
19 * python -c "from cryptography import x509; \
20 * c=x509.load_pem_x509_certificate(open('CERT.crt','rb').read()); \
21 * n=c.public_key().public_numbers(); \
22 * print(hex(n.x)); print(hex(n.y))"
23 ******************************************************************/
